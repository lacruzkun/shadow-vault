## Objectives of the Study

The main objective of this project is to design and implement a multi-vendor e-commerce website for small business owners with integrated inventory management.

The specific objectives are to:

1. Design and implement a secure user management system that supports customer, vendor, and administrator accounts with role-based access control.
    
2. Develop an administrator module that enables the review and manual approval of vendor registrations before they are permitted to sell products on the platform.
    
3. Design and implement a vendor dashboard that allows approved vendors to add, edit, delete, and manage their products and inventory.
    
4. Develop a customer-facing marketplace that enables users to browse, search, and purchase products from multiple vendors through a unified shopping cart and checkout system.
    
5. Implement an online payment module that allows customers to complete purchases securely through supported payment methods.
    
6. Design and implement an inventory management system that automatically updates stock levels after successful purchases and notifies vendors when inventory reaches predefined low-stock thresholds.
    
7. Develop an order management and delivery tracking system that allows administrators and vendors to monitor and update order statuses from order placement to delivery.
    
8. Implement a notification system that informs customers and vendors of important events such as order confirmations, payment status, delivery progress, and inventory alerts.
    
9. Develop an administrative management module that enables the administrator to monitor platform activities, manage users and products, and manually resolve disputes between customers and vendors.
